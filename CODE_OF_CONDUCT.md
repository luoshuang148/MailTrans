# Community guidelines

Everyone is welcome to participate in MailTrans, regardless of experience, background, or preferred language.

- Be respectful and constructive. Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people.
- Give newcomers enough context to reproduce and understand a problem.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, spam, or share someone else's private information.
- Protect email privacy: use synthetic examples, redact screenshots, and follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

For conduct concerns, flag the relevant public comment using GitHub's reporting tools or request maintainer help in the relevant discussion without repeating private information. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome.

欢迎所有人参与。请尊重他人、围绕问题讨论，不骚扰、不歧视、不泄露隐私。邮件案例使用人工内容，截图清理个人信息。行为问题可通过 GitHub 举报功能或在相关讨论中请求维护者处理，避免重复传播私人信息。
